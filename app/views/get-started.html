Get started with GOV.UK Wallet

GOV.UK Wallet is an app that lets you securely save your government documents to your phone.

You can use these documents to prove things such as your age, identity or eligibility for services.

Use of GOV.UK Wallet is entirely optional.

How to get GOV.UK Wallet

On a smartphone, you can download the GOV.UK Wallet app from the:

  • App Store on an iPhone
  • Google Play Store on an Android phone

If you’re using an iPhone, it must be:

  • an iPhone 8 or newer
  • running iOS 16.7 or higher

For an Android phone (for example, Samsung or Google Pixel), it must be running Android 10 or higher.

Documents you can add

You must already hold a government document to add it to your GOV.UK Wallet.

You can currently add a digital version of your:

  • UK driving licence, if you live in selected postcode areas in Aberdeen, Cardiff and Newcastle
  • HM Armed Forces Veteran Card

In future you’ll be able to add more government documents to the app.

What you cannot add

You can only add government documents to GOV.UK Wallet. You cannot add other things, like payment cards, boarding passes or concert tickets.

You can only add your own documents. You cannot add documents that belong to other people, such as members of your family.
