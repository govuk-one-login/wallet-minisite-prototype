GOV.UK Wallet: your docs & ID

Prove your identity with GOV.UK One Login, then save and share documents

GOV.UK Wallet helps you prove things about yourself using government-issued documents like your driving licence, securely stored on your phone.

The app can also be used to prove your identity for GOV.UK One Login, used to sign in to many government services.
